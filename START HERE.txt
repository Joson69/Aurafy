Aurafy 0.8.0 for Windows x64

1. Extract the ZIP to a folder and open aurafy.exe. No build tools are needed.
2. To connect Spotify, create your own app at https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard
   and add these Redirect URIs:
   http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback
   http://127.0.0.1:43821/callback
3. In Aurafy, open Settings > Account, enter the app's Client ID, then choose
   Save and connect Spotify. Sign in with your own account.
4. For audio directly in Aurafy, get your own Soloist key at
   https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard/soloist. In Settings > Account >
   Local player connection, enter the key and select Save key & enable playback.
   Aurafy downloads the playback engine if needed. If prompted to pair, select
   Aurafy in the Spotify device picker on your phone on the same network.

This package contains the app, these instructions, and license notices.
